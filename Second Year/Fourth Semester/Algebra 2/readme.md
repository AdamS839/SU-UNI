---

## Grade Formation

Just like **Linear Algebra** in the first semester, your final grade is determined by a combination of practical and theoretical evaluations.

### 1. In-Semester Control Exams
* There are **3 control exams** held throughout the semester.
* **The Exemption Rule:** If you pass every control exam (grade $\ge 3$) and your overall average across them is **at least 4.5**, you can skip the practical exam entirely and proceed straight to the theory.

### 2. The Theoretical Exam
The theory exam consists of two parts:
1. **Written Part:** Writing out a complete chapter from the course material.
2. **Oral Part:** An in-depth discussion and conversation with the associate professor or assistant on a second chapter.

### Final Grade Calculation
If you successfully pass both parts of the theoretical exam, your final grade is calculated as the average of three components:

$$\text{Final Grade} = \frac{\text{Practical Exam} + \text{Theoretical Part 1} + \text{Theoretical Part 2}}{3}$$
